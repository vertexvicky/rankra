About Rankra

Updated: April 25, 2026

Our Mission

Rankra is an independent educational data platform built with a single goal: to empower students in Tamil Nadu with clear, accurate, and actionable admission insights.

We believe that high-quality data should be accessible to everyone. By transforming complex official records into a fast, intuitive experience, we help thousands of engineering aspirants make data-driven decisions about their future with precision.

Personalized Experience

With our latest update, Rankra now offers **User Accounts**. By creating a profile, you can personalize your experience based on your specific role (Student or Teacher), community, and preferred medium. This allows us to serve you more relevant cutoff data and institution insights instantly upon login.

Data Integrity

Integrity is the foundation of Rankra. We don't guess at numbers; we derive them from source material:

  • TNEA Official Records: Our cutoff engines are built directly from official round-wise data and updated PDFs from the Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions portal, spanning from 2020 to 2025.
  • College & Course Insights: Information regarding campus infrastructure and branch prospects is meticulously collected from verified official sites, direct student reviews, and on-ground feedback.

An Indie Initiative

Rankra is not a corporate-backed entity. We are an indie project born out of a passion for transparency in the education sector. Operating from Thiruvarur, Tamil Nadu, we are deeply connected to the student community we serve. Every feature, from our custom search engine to our profile system, is designed with the user's journey in mind.

Technical Excellence

We pride ourselves on building tools that are as fast as they are useful. Our platform uses custom-built prefix-tree algorithms for real-time search, Firestore for secure profile management, and advanced XOR-based data encryption to ensure your insights are both fast and secure.
