Seat Distribution & Management Quota

In accordance with government regulations, approximately **65% of seats** in private engineering colleges are filled through TNEA counselling (Government Quota), while the remaining **35%** are available via Management Quota (Direct Admission).

Government Institutions: Please note that 100% of seats in Government and Government-Aided colleges are filled strictly through TNEA counselling. These institutions **do not** offer Management Quota seats.

We strongly advise all students to thoroughly check the previous years' cutoffs on Rankra for their PCM marks before committing to a management quota seat. Understanding your merit-based admission chances can help you make an informed financial and educational decision.

Financial Aid & Scholarship Eligibility

Certain critical financial benefits and government schemes are **strictly reserved** for students who secure admission through TNEA counselling (Government Quota). These include:

  • First Graduate Scholarship: Tuition fee concessions for first-generation graduates.
  • 7.5% Horizontal Reservation: For students who studied in government schools (6th to 12th).
  • Post Matric Scholarship: For eligible SC/ST/SCA/Converted Christian students.
  • Quota Benefits: Sports, Ex-Servicemen, and Differently Abled reservations.

Students opting for Management Quota (Direct Admission) are generally **ineligible** for these government-sponsored scholarship schemes.
