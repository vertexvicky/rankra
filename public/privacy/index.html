Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

Our Commitment

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. At Rankra, we follow a strictly privacy-first approach. We collect only the data necessary to provide you with a high-quality, personalized experience for your admission research.

Guest Access & Anonymous Usage

Rankra allows users to explore the platform without creating an account (Guest Mode). During Guest sessions:

  • Local Usage Tracking: We use localStorage on your device to maintain a count of your search filter applications. This is done solely to enforce fair usage limits for the free version of the platform.
  • No Personal Data: In Guest Mode, we do not collect or store any personal information such as names or emails.

User Accounts & Profiles

To unlock unlimited searches and personalized cutoff insights, Rankra requires users to create a profile. We collect and store the following:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and authentication provider (Google or Email/Password).
  • Profile Preferences: Your educational role (Student/Teacher), community, school type, and preferred medium of instruction.
  • Storage: This data is securely stored in Firebase Firestore. We also cache this profile locally on your device (using localStorage) to ensure the platform remains fast and responsive on subsequent visits.

Cookies & Third-Party Services

Rankra uses industry-standard authentication and advertising services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Used to securely manage your login sessions and protect your profile data.
  • Google AdSense: To support this free project, we display ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including Firebase's secure infrastructure, to protect your account data. Our core educational data files are encrypted and compressed to prevent unauthorized scraping and ensure data integrity for all users.

Contact & Data Deletion

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us at help@rankra.in.
