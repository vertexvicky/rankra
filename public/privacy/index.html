Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 22, 2026

Our Commitment

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. At Rankra, we follow a strictly privacy-first approach. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide you with a high-quality experience.

Data Collection

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our cutoff search tools. However, we may collect the following information:

  • Preference Data: We store your selected community (e.g., OC, BC) and theme preference (Light/Dark) locally on your device (using localStorage) so you don't have to re-select them every time you visit.
  • Contact Form Data: If you voluntarily reach out via our contact form, we collect your name and email address to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies & Third-Party Services

Rankra itself does not use tracking cookies. However, we use third-party services that may use cookies:

  • Google AdSense: To support this free project, we may display ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect the data we handle. Our core data files are compressed and encrypted to prevent unauthorized access and ensure integrity.
